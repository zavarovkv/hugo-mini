Welcome to Mini — a fast, minimal Hugo blog theme. This is the example site demonstrating the theme's features.

Mini is built for writers and makers who want a clean, distraction-free blog. No JavaScript frameworks, no build pipelines, no external dependencies — just content, typography, and speed.

The theme supports [dark mode](/blog/), [math formulas](/math-demo/), [Mermaid diagrams](/mermaid-demo/), and [multilingual content](/ru/) out of the box. Everything is self-hosted: fonts, styles, scripts. Your readers never talk to a CDN.

Check out the [articles](/blog/) to see the theme in action.
