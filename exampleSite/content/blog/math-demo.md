Mini supports math formulas via KaTeX. Enable it per-page with `math = true` in front matter.

## Inline Math

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.

## Block Math

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$
